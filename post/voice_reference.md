Companies want senior engineers, but they’re dismantling the pipeline that produces them.

Recently watched a great talk by Laurie Voss (co-founder of npm) on the AI-driven rise of the “Product Engineer.” Laurie offers the pictured practical takeaways for engineers navigating this shift.

As AI makes writing code increasingly cheap, engineering value shifts toward:

1. Problem identification and scoping
2. Specifying and architecting solutions
3. Thorough validation *where it matters most*

Field engineering reinforces this. My most valuable learning experiences have focused on ambiguous problems, working with customers, and owning solutions from A to Z.

If engineering judgment is becoming more valuable, we need to invest in opportunities to develop it.

Give junior engineers ownership, customer exposure, and mentorship. That’s how we develop engineers who know not just how to build, but what’s worth building.
